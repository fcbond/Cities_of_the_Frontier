# Cities of the Frontier User Guide

## Iron

Iron is a stored resource used alongside gold to construct most buildings. Your
current supply is shown in the kingdom status.

### Building mines

1. Move a **Peasant Workers** unit onto an ordinary mountain hex.
2. Right-click the workers and select **Build a Mine**.
3. Keep the workers there for the four work-shifts needed to finish the mine.

A mine costs 50 gold. Its initial iron cost depends on difficulty:

| Difficulty | Iron cost |
| --- | ---: |
| Easy | 0 |
| Normal | 1 |
| Hard | 2 |

Mines can be started on normal mountains in spring or autumn and on dry
mountains in summer. Snow-covered winter mountains and impassable mountain
peaks cannot be mined.

If the menu option does not appear, check that the selected unit is **Peasant
Workers**, the workers are not already assigned to a project, and the hex is an
ordinary mountain rather than an impassable peak.

### Mine production

Completed mines produce iron at the start of each new season:

| Mine staffing at the end of the season | Iron produced |
| --- | ---: |
| Empty | 2 |
| Any Peasant or Peasant Workers unit | 3 |
| Dwarvish Miner | 5 |

Only the unit standing on the mine when the season ends affects production.
For the best output, move a Dwarvish Miner onto each mine before the final turn
of the season.

### Dwarvish Miners

Send an Envoy to the northern signpost to contact the dwarves. If relations
permit it, ask them to teach you how to dig more iron from the mountains. This
unlocks recruitment of **Dwarvish Miners**.

### Flattening hills

For immediate iron, move Peasant Workers onto an unforested hill, right-click,
and select **Flatten hills**. The project takes four work-shifts and produces
1 iron when completed. It costs 8 gold on Easy and 10 gold on Normal or Hard.
Unlike mines, this removes the hill and does not provide recurring production.
